A plan's members

For a workspace you host for others on a plan, --member-notice <n> says how many members the plan includes. Once the workspace has more, its owner and administrators see a line under the conversation's header saying so, and Workspace settings → General → Plan says the same for as long as it is true. --plan-name names the plan in it ("the Team plan"), and --upgrade-url adds a See plans link that opens in the browser. Without them the line says "its plan" and links nowhere.

ownbase-server --data ./data --member-notice 50 --plan-name Team \
  --upgrade-url "https://example.com/plans?workspace=acme"

It is a notice, never a limit: nobody is locked out, and people keep joining and signing in past it. Members and guests are not told. People with an account who can sign in are counted; apps, guests and deactivated accounts are not. Not now puts the line away on that device for two weeks; the Plan section stays. The settings are also OWNBASE_MEMBER_NOTICE, OWNBASE_PLAN_NAME and OWNBASE_UPGRADE_URL. The server prints the plan's members when it starts, and a name or an address without --member-notice is refused.
