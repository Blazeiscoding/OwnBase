# Individual contributor license agreement

> **Draft for legal review.** This text has not been checked by a lawyer and is not yet
> in force. It follows the terms of the Apache Software Foundation's individual
> contributor license agreement, in our own words. "[Licensor]" stands for the legal
> entity that will maintain OwnBase, to be named once it exists. Until a reviewed text
> replaces this one, contributions are accepted under the license of the part of the
> repository they change, and nothing more.

This agreement is between you and [Licensor]. It covers everything you submit to the
OwnBase project (a "contribution"): code, documentation, pictures and anything else,
in any part of the repository, including `ee/`.

## 1. Copyright

You keep the copyright in your contributions. You give [Licensor], and anyone who
receives software from it, a worldwide, non-exclusive, free, irrevocable license to
copy, change, publicly show and perform, sublicense and distribute your contributions
and works based on them, under any license, open source or not. This is what lets the
project release the MIT core and the Enterprise edition, and change either license
later.

## 2. Patents

You give [Licensor], and anyone who receives software from it, a worldwide,
non-exclusive, free, irrevocable license under any patent you can license that a
contribution of yours would infringe, on its own or combined with the project as it
was when you made it, to make, use, sell, offer, import and otherwise transfer it. If
anyone claims in court that a contribution of yours, or the project, infringes a
patent, the patent license they had from this agreement ends on the day they make
the claim.

## 3. What you promise

- Each contribution is your own original work, or you say clearly where it is not,
  with the details of its license and its author.
- You are allowed to make this agreement. If your employer or anyone else has rights
  in what you make, they have agreed in writing to your contributing it, or have
  given up those rights for it.
- You will tell the project if any of this stops being true.

## 4. What you do not promise

You give your contributions as they are, without warranty or support of any kind,
unless you choose to offer them in writing.

## How to agree

Pull requests are checked by the CLA Assistant: the first time you open one, it asks
you to agree by leaving a comment. You agree once, for all your contributions.
